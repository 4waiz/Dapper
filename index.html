        Policy race

        same paired scenario, same execution model

        Every policy replays the identical immutable trace through the identical cost model, so a decision selects which outcome is realised but cannot change the random future. The oracle reads the realised outcome of the current frame and is not deployable; it is here to bound what perfect knowledge of the next instant would buy.

        Where DAPPER does not win

        DAPPER · Built by Awaiz Ahmed · research artifact for IEEE FMEC 2026 · source
        Controlled synthetic network study with a secondary measured-detector experiment. Confidence values in the main tables are simulated proxies, never detector accuracy. Nothing here ran on a robot or on embedded hardware, no energy was instrumented, and degraded-safe names a conservative fallback mode, not a certified safety property.
