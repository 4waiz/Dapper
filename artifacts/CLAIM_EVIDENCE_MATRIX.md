## Wording that must not appear in the camera-ready paper

* "25 KB versus 9,150 KB" or any bandwidth figure from the published tables - superseded by the corrected accounting.
* "DAPPER achieves 0 % deadline misses" without scoping it to D = 100 ms and to the evaluated synthetic profiles.
* "accuracy" for any number from the synthetic benchmark - it is a confidence proxy.
* "real-world", "physical robot", "Jetson", "embedded" or "measured wireless" anywhere in the results.
* "safe", "guarantees safety" or "certified" for the degraded-safe mode.
* "negligible overhead" - quote the measured microseconds instead.
* "significantly outperformed" without naming the test that was performed.
* "robust to +-20 % (or +-30 %) estimation error" - the measured response is asymmetric; quote the directional interval instead.
